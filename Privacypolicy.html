Privacy Policy & Studio Rules

Official documentation for NomaByte Studio website, Tester Portal, and mobile applications.

01

About NomaByte Studio & Website

NomaByte Studio is an independent mobile software development studio based in Bratislava, Slovakia. We specialize in building cross-platform mobile applications for Android and iOS using modern technologies (Flutter, Dart, Firebase).

Our official website (nomabyte.com) serves as a portfolio showcase, technical support hub, and entry point for our closed beta-testing community. When you submit a contact form on our site, your name and email address are processed securely via Formspree solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

Tester Portal & Data Handling

Access to the NomaByte Tester Portal (dashboard.html) is restricted to authorized beta-testers approved by our team.

Confidentiality (NDA): Any APK files, TestFlight links, screenshots, or feature concepts available inside the Tester Portal are confidential. Testers agree not to leak or share unpublished builds publicly prior to official release.

Bug Reports: Screenshots and device logs uploaded via the bug report form are transmitted directly to our development team to diagnose and fix software issues.

04

App Privacy Policy: Vodičák SK

Google Play Official

Vodičák SK is an educational driving school exam simulator designed for users preparing for theoretical driving tests in Slovakia.

  • Authentication & Progress: The app uses anonymous authentication via Firebase Authentication to save your test progress, statistics, and streak data without requiring personal identification.
  • Third-Party Services: To keep the application accessible and stable, we integrate Google AdMob (for displaying ads) and Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (for anonymous crash reporting and performance monitoring). These services may collect device identifiers and anonymous usage metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Data Deletion: Since progress is tied to an anonymous session or local storage, clearing the app data or uninstalling the application removes local records. For any data inquiries, contact us directly.
